# Data use

The phenotype measurements and derived analysis tables in this repository are provided for research reproducibility and may be reused with citation of the associated manuscript and this repository.

The original RNA-sequencing data and transcriptome-derived resources remain subject to the terms of their source repository and publication. See NCBI BioProject PRJNA912645 and Barratt *et al.* (2023), doi:10.1111/tpj.16248.

No additional rights are asserted over third-party source data.
